What the panels say together.

Section 1 reads like a soft landing. Positive growth, contained unemployment, inflation slightly above target. The cycle looks alive. Section 2 tells you why that reading is incomplete. The fiscal arithmetic is deteriorating regardless of cycle outcomes. Interest expense alone now exceeds half a trillion dollars annually and is rising. Government credit creation has carried the economy since 2008.

Section 3 shows the structural balance sheet. Net debtor at -80% of GDP (partial recovery from -90% trough on dollar weakness). Foreign UST share down from 49% to 22% in fifteen years. The exorbitant privilege crossed zero in 2026 for the first time in fifty years. Sections 4 and 5 show how the system is pricing this. The dollar still strong against other fiat but pulled back from the 2025 peak. Reserve share declining steadily. Gold up sharply against the dollar. Oil spiking. The market is voting on debasement risk, not growth risk.

The structural read: the United States is not at risk of default or hyperinflation. It is at risk of the slow unwinding — a multi-decade adjustment through the currency rather than through the budget. The cyclical data will dominate the next news cycle. The external account will dominate the next decade.
